Full Moon Healing Circles

A circle held under the full moon, once a month, for release, reflection, and community connection. A space to set down what no longer serves you and step into what's next.

Held once a month, sometimes twice when a blue moon calls for it.

What to expect

Ritual, not routine

Each circle follows the moon, not a fixed weekly schedule. That's intentional. The full moon has long been a marker for release and reflection, and this circle is built around that rhythm rather than around convenience.

Beginner-friendly

No prior experience with meditation or healing circles needed. You'll be gently guided every step of the way.

Sharing is optional

You're invited to participate in whatever way feels safe and supportive. No one is required to speak.

Guided reflection & sound

Expect guided reflection, journaling, optional sharing, and a restorative sound bath to support release and integration.

All backgrounds welcome

This is a spiritual and reflective space, not a religious one, open to people of all backgrounds, beliefs, and identities.
