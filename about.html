About Jess

We know what it's like to enter a room and feel the need to become smaller.

Jessica Metayer founded Jess Breathe and Flow to create the culturally affirming spaces she needed but never found, where people of color can drop the mask, breathe deep, and be who they were always meant to be.

Credentials & experience

  • MS in Organization Development, Pepperdine Graziadio
  • 500-hour yoga teacher training
  • Graduate research with 15 Black executive leaders
  • Consulting engagements with Workday, International Justice Mission (Washington, DC), and the City of Lyon Ecological Transition Team, where she served as lead facilitator
  • Four years facilitating healing circles in community
  • 17 community and civic partners
