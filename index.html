The weight you're carrying

You've done the therapy, the readings, the podcasts. You're still stretched thin.

Pressure to lead, provide, and hold it all together, without space to fall apart or be witnessed. Disconnection from your body, your emotions, your intuitive wisdom. The sense that you're on the cusp of transformation, but missing the tools, safety, or container to break through.

Wellness spaces weren't built with you in mind. Healing shouldn't require you to shrink, translate, or audition for belonging.
